Apparel sites miss key UX standards

Across Sri Lanka, independent fashion ateliers often begin on Instagram or Facebook—yet relying solely on social feeds leaves customers guessing about true fabric colors, real measurements, and return policies. Empirical research on small-enterprise websites shows 97.1% visitor dissatisfaction with passive brochure portals, whereas active transactional stores achieve 85.3% satisfaction.

STYLE WEAR bridges local craft and global UX standards. By pairing Sri Lankan mobile-first checkout (PayHere, FriMi, bank transfers, and Cash on Delivery) with Baymard-validated sizing clarity, unedited garment visuals, and interactive 3D outfitting, small-scale apparel makers can compete directly with established houses while drastically reducing costly product returns.
